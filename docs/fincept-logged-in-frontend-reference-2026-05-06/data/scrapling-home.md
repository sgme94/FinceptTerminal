Everything For Professional Market Analysis
-------------------------------------------

Trusted by portfolio managers, quantitative researchers, and macro analysts. Real-time market data, advanced analytics, and institutional charting across global markets.

### Real-Time Market Data

NASDAQ, NYSE, AMEX, OTC, CBOE — 19,000+ instruments with real-time OHLCV data, the same coverage investment banks and hedge funds demand.

### AI Market Assistant

GenAI-powered research assistant for portfolio managers and analysts. Query earnings, analyze macro trends, and generate investment insights in natural language.

### Institutional Charting

50+ technical indicators including RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands. Multi-timeframe analysis and candlestick patterns used by professional traders.

### Global Economic Intelligence

GDP, inflation, bond yields, and 100+ economic indicators. Essential for economists, macro strategists, and geopolitical analysts tracking global markets.

### Real-Time News

Aggregated financial news with sentiment analysis. Stay ahead of market-moving events — critical for fund managers and active traders.

### Equity Research

Income statements, balance sheets, cash flow, valuation ratios, and profitability metrics — the same fundamental analysis workflow used at family offices and investment firms.
